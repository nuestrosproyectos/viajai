Questions people ask

Is this an official service?

No. Paperbridge is a private company. We are not part of any government and the official fees above are the same ones you'd pay on your own.

Can I do it myself?

Yes. You can order the certificate on GOV.UK, send it to the FCDO for the apostille and find a sworn translator in Spain. We do all three for you, with one quote and one point of contact.

Will the Spanish office accept the translation?

Sworn translations by translators appointed by Spain's Ministry of Foreign Affairs are accepted by Spanish authorities. Some offices also ask for recent certificates; tell Andrés your procedure and province and we'll check.

How long does it take?

It depends on the register, the apostille service and the post. We give you a realistic timeline with your quote, before you pay.

Is Andrés a real person?

No. Andrés is an AI assistant that explains the process and takes your request. A person from our team reviews every request and sends your quote.
